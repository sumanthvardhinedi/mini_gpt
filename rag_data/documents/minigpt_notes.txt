MiniGPT Architecture, Training Experiment, and Generation Summary

MiniGPT (`model/gpt.py`) is a decoder-only GPT language model built from scratch in Python and PyTorch for educational clarity. The full architecture consists of InputEmbedding (TokenEmbedding + PositionalEmbedding), a stack of `num_layers` causal TransformerBlock modules, a final LayerNorm (`ln_f`), and a linear language model head (`lm_head`) whose weight matrix is tied directly to the token embedding weights (`self.lm_head.weight = self.embeddings.token_embedding.embedding.weight`).

In the Phase 10 reproducible training experiment (`configs/small_gpt.json`), MiniGPT is configured with `vocab_size = 96`, `sequence_length = 12`, `max_sequence_length = 32`, `embedding_dim = 16`, `num_heads = 4`, `num_layers = 1`, and `hidden_dim = 32`. The model has 4,304 trainable parameters (`weight-tied`) and is trained for 5 epochs on CPU using the AdamW optimizer (`learning_rate = 0.04`, `weight_decay = 0.01`, `seed = 42`). Across 5 epochs (24.05 seconds on CPU), training loss decreases from 4.7427 to 4.6037 and validation loss decreases from 4.5870 to 4.5264, reducing validation perplexity from 98.20 to 92.43.

During Phase 9 and Phase 11 autoregressive text generation (`generation/generate.py`), MiniGPT runs in `model.eval()` and `torch.no_grad()` mode, supporting deterministic greedy decoding (`torch.argmax`), temperature scaling (`logits / temperature`), top-k sampling (`-inf` masking outside the top K logits), and context-window cropping (`input_ids[:, -max_sequence_length:]`).
